Important Ministry (MTO) Regulations

Please read these mandatory requirements to successfully claim your BDE certificate.

01

1. Course Completion Time Limit

The Ministry of Transportation (MTO) requires all students to complete all classroom, homework, and in-car training modules within ONE YEAR OF THE STARTING DATE. No exceptions are permitted by MTO.

02

2. Certification Requirements

To qualify for official MTO certification, all students must complete at least 20 hours of classroom learning (or online BDE theory), 10 hours of homework learning links, and 10 hours of 1-on-1 practical in-car training.

03

3. Road Test Waiting Period Reduction

Once our driving school certifies you on the MTO portal, you qualify to take your G2 road test early—in just 8 months instead of the standard 12 months. No manual Driver's Licence History search is required.
